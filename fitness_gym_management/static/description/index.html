✨ Key Features

The Gym Management module provides a comprehensive fitness center management platform with complete workflow management, role-based access control, and integrated reporting capabilities for efficient gym operations.

👥

Member Management

Complete member registration with auto-reference (GM0001), profile tracking, contact details, photo upload, and membership history overview.

💳

Membership Management

Flexible membership types with automatic status computation (active/inactive/expired), purchase wizard, renewal management, and auto invoice generation.

👨‍🏫

Trainer Management

Trainer profiles with skill tracking, specialization, member assignment, trainer fee management, and auto-reference (TN0001).

🏃

Exercise Management

Complete exercise library with name, duration, calories, benefits, steps, video links, equipment requirements, and body part categorization.

📋

Workout Plan Management

Create workout plans with exercise assignments per day of week, sets, reps, calorie tracking, date ranges, and automatic total computation.

🥗

Diet Plan Management

Personalized diet plans with food items, meal intervals, weekly schedule, quantity-based calorie calculation, and comprehensive reporting.

📊

BMI & BMR Calculation

Automatic BMI and BMR (Mifflin-St Jeor formula) calculations based on height, weight, age, and gender with historical health records.

⚙️

Comprehensive Configuration

Extensive master data configuration: trainer skills, body parts, workout days, categories, exercise types, diet intervals, equipment, and food items.

🔐

Role-Based Access Control

Three dedicated user groups — Admin, Trainer, and User — with record-level security rules ensuring trainers see only their assigned members.

📄

Advanced Reporting

Three QWeb PDF reports: Member Profile, Workout Plan Schedule, and Diet Plan — providing full visibility into gym operations and member progress.

🧾

Billing & Invoice Management

Automatic invoice generation for membership fees and trainer fees, seamlessly integrated with Odoo's accounting module.

🔔

Membership Renewal Reminders

Automated email reminders for membership renewals. Send notifications to members before their membership expires to avoid churn.

🎧

50 Days Free Support

Dedicated support team to ensure smooth adoption, quick issue resolution, and a seamless experience after purchase.
